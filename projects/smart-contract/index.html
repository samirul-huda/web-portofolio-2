Blockchain-Based Diploma Verification

This project is a decentralized system (DApp) built using Smart Contract (Solidity) to record and verify diplomas in a transparent, secure, and immutable manner. This system involves the roles of the Owner (Main Admin), the University (Diploma Issuer), and the Student.

1. General & Public Verification

A public homepage and verification system where third parties (e.g., a company's HR department) can validate the authenticity of a diploma simply by scanning the barcode or searching based on the validation ID input.

Landing Page

The main page introduces the Blockchain-based diploma recording system.

Login System

Secure digital wallet integration authentication for each role: Owner, University, and Student.

Diploma Scan Page

Where companies/the public can scan diplomas to verify their authenticity.

Diploma Validation: Valid

The status will appear valid when the diploma data has been successfully verified to match the immutable data in the Smart Contract.

2. Owner Flow (System Admin)

As Root/Superadmin (Kemenristekdikti or an authorized body), the Owner is responsible for registering institutions colleges or universities to have the authority to issue certificates/diplomas on the Blockchain.

Dashboard Owner

The main administrative information center for all participants in the Blockchain system.

List of Universities

Which university data systems are officially registered in smart contracts?

Add New University

A central action to authorize access rights for issuing university-owned wallet addresses.

Change Wallet Owner

An essential feature of Smart Contracts is transferring system ownership to another administrator's wallet if security mitigation is needed.

Smart Contract Logs

Monitoring the activity history of every wallet interaction executed on the blockchain network.

3. University Flow (Institusi)

The campus administration (university administrator) is responsible for recording campus attributes such as faculties and study programs, drafting graduate diplomas, and then publishing (minting) these diplomas to the blockchain.

University Dashboard

The main page of each university that has received wallet validation for issuance.

Add Faculty

Registration of faculties under the auspices of the college.

Faculty List

Recapitulation list of all existing faculties.

Add Study Program

Fill in the study program details for each registered faculty.

List of Study Programs

This list of study programs is an important link in the process of printing student certificates later.

Student Diploma Draft

The initial process of registering the identity of a diploma holder by a university before its network is validated.

Publish to Blockchain

Providing final sign and absolute recording of student diploma drafts into the Smart Contract ecosystem.

List of University Diplomas

Contains a comprehensive history of all diplomas and transcripts has been published by the agency to the Blockchain.

Diploma Execution Details

Examine the detailed recording of a diploma and its verification hash code.

4. Student Flow

As the owner of their digital diploma, students can view, print (digitally using a barcode), and verify that the data recorded on the Blockchain is identical to the original without any modification by hackers.

Student Dashboard

Graduation dashboard profile view for student wallet holders.

Internal Data Validity

Verify the completeness of your graduate ID and diploma before using them to apply for a job.

Authenticated E-Diploma Display

The form of a digital diploma document and an authentic hashed QR code can be attached or downloaded by the user.
