Company Payroll System

This project is a comprehensive Payroll System developed using CodeIgniter 4, PHP, and MySQL. It streamlines Human Resource processes including attendance management, overtime tracking, payroll calculation, and complaint handling. The system features two main roles: Owner/HR and Employee (Karyawan).

1. System Authentication

Secure login portal for both employees and owners to access their respective dashboards and features based on their role credentials.

Login Portal

Main gateway to access the payroll system securely.

2. Employee Flow (Karyawan)

Employees can manage their daily attendance through coordinate tracking and selfies, apply for overtime, check their salary and download pay slips, and communicate grievances directly with management.

Employee Dashboard

Overview of personal attendance stats, recent salaries, and quick performance metrics.

Attendance Entry

Daily check-in and check-out interface for employees.

Live Coordinates Capture

Captures precise geographical location to prevent false attendance claims.

Selfie Capture

Integrating direct camera access ensuring the employee's physical presence.

Attendance History

Complete log of check-in and check-out times accessible by the employee.

Overtime Records

Tracking all approved and pending overtime hours directly affecting payroll calculations.

Overtime Form

Submission form for employees requesting extra paid task hours.

Salary History

Record of monthly disbursed salaries throughout their employment.

Salary Slip Detail

Breakdown of gross salary, deductions, bonuses, and net pay that can be exported physically.

Complaints List

Monitor resolutions for reported issues or internal HR grievances.

Submit Complaint

Directly message HR about discrepancies or workplace inconveniences.

3. Owner (HR) Flow

The Owner/HR acts as the central administrator configuring base salaries per position, evaluating attendances, confirming overtime requests, disbursing payrolls, and generating actionable reports.

Owner Dashboard

Comprehensive real-time insight into total workforce status, live attendance metrics, and payroll finances.

Manage Employees

Complete list of registered personnel with easy update and delete functions.

Add New Employee

Set up new employee accounts, assigning roles, contact info, and system credentials.

Manage Positions

Creating tiered organizational structures like Managers, Staff, or Interns.

Setting Base Salary

Determine basic salary limits and recurrent allowances dynamically tied to specific job titles.

Process Salaries

Initiate automated calculations combining base salaries, overtime rates, and deductions.

Manage Overtime Claims

Approve or reject employee overtime forms which dynamically modify the final monthly checks.

Bonus Configuration

Assign special rewards for high-achieving personnel for a given period manually.

Employee Attendance Log

Check and investigate specific daily punch-in times and their validity geo-locations.

HQ Announcements

Broadcast administrative information uniformly to all employee dashboard interfaces rapidly.

Employee Grievances

Reply and resolve internal disputes submitted directly by the employees smoothly.

Attendance Output Report

Filter periods and download formal HR attendance data as printable documents.

Salary Output Report

Summarize total budget allocations resolving monthly accounting tasks gracefully.

Admin Profile Settings

Update company credentials, user password, and administrator account specifics.
